How To Play

Movement: Use W, A, S, D to navigate the dungeon arenas.

Combat Combo: Left-click repeatedly to perform a 3-hit melee combo. The 3rd hit deals massive AoE damage.

Dash & I-Frames: Press Spacebar to dash quickly through enemy attacks with complete invulnerability.

Active Skills: Press 1 for Slash Wave (ranged beam) and 2 for Meteor Strike (devastating AOE).

Shop & Armory: Buy new swords and armor with gold earned from abyss runs before entering.
